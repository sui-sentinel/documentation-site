## The Skills That Matter

AI agents will soon manage your finances, health data, and work. The people who understand how they
fail will have a real edge in security, in AI engineering, and in the job market.

Red Sentinel is where those skills are forged, in live adversarial competition with real money on
the line.

## Two Ways to Compete

- **Attack:** Browse live Sentinels. Find the weakness. Break through and claim the entire prize pool. Every attempt builds your on-chain reputation.
- **Defend:** Deploy a Sentinel. Stake the prize pool. Earn 40% of every attack fee while your AI holds. Survive 14 days and withdraw everything, plus proof your system can't be broken.

## Why It Works

- **Real Stakes:** Real money on the line. The stakes make you take it seriously, and that's how skills develop.
- **Verified Results:** Every attack judged inside a Trusted Execution Environment. No disputes. No committees. If you broke through, you win. Instantly.
- **Portable Proof:** Your attack history is on-chain. Verifiable. Permanent. Not claims on a resume, but proof of what you've actually done.

## The Red Sentinel Solution

Attackers pay a small fee to challenge live AI Sentinels. If they discover a verified exploit, they earn an instant reward from the bounty pool. Failed attacks grow the bounty and reward defenders, creating a real-stakes training loop where skill, persistence, and security judgment matter.

## How It Works

- **Defenders:** Deploy their AI as a Sentinel, define instructions, fund a reward pool. Earn 40% of every attack fee. Survive 14 days unbroken and keep everything.
- **Attackers:** Pay a small message fee to attempt jailbreaks, data extraction, and adversarial attacks. Break a Sentinel, claim the entire bounty pool instantly.
- **The Protocol:** A DSPY-powered jury model evaluates each attack inside a TEE. Results are cryptographically attested and settled on-chain automatically.

## Economics

Each message fee splits three ways: 50% → reward pool, 40% → defender, 10% → protocol. Failed attacks grow the bounty, attracting stronger attackers over time. Defenders also earn continuous Sentinel token rewards proportional to their pool size.
